What you are looking at

TerrainLEGO brick columns: a top plate with studs on grass, earth courses under hills, topsoil then soil and rock courses on every exposed cliff down to the sea. Paths sit a plate lower, ponds have a sand bed under glass water.
Land joinsBetween neighbours: ragged coast, a path on the old bridge lane, hills, ponds and trees. Colours blend from one area to the other.
HighlandsRise in each block of four open areas: grass foothills, rock slopes, snow only on the top level.
SeaReef shelf in three depths with coral, a foam line that breathes, waves and sun glints, fish schools, shark fins, a whale that surfaces and spouts, jellyfish that glow at night.
Day cycle24 minutes per day in game. Warm sunrise and sunset, a bluish moonlit night, lanterns and glow parts light up, fireflies over the grass.
Area lifeWindmill sails turn, leaves fall in the Forest, sand drifts in the Desert, sparkles rise in the Echo Field, sparks at the Mine, waterfalls off the Bridge and the Park, seagulls over the coast, cloud shadows drift across the land.
