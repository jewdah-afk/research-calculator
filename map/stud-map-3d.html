Stud Incremental 3D Map

A 3D block-out of the whole map using the in-game names and icons. Every generator and pad is drawn as a "machine" in the style of the Crystal Core and Stack concepts: a dark base with glowing strips, two side pillars, and the currency's object floating in the middle with its icon above it. Drag to spin, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. Click any zone, or pick one from the list, to fly there and read its build brief. Pads that reset progress are marked with a red hazard ring.

Machine style (every pad and generator)

  • Base: dark slate (#262E3D) block with chamfered edges, about 20×14 studs, 3 studs tall. A slightly lighter top step (#2F384A) sits on it.
  • Glow strips: two Neon strips on the front face of the base, in the currency's colour.
  • Pillars: two dark pillars, one on each side, each with a tall Neon panel on the front. Core style (Gems, Lighting, Neon, Glass…) puts angled caps on top that lean in over the centre, like the Crystal Core. Stack style (Stack, Bricks, Welds, Crates…) uses stepped blocks, like the Stack concept.
  • Centre: an octagonal dark pedestal with a glowing ring, and the currency's object floating above it (slow spin, ±1 stud bob). The object is a 3D version of the icon: a red 2×4 brick for Bricks, a purple gem for Gems, a pink ball for Balls, and so on.
  • Icon billboard: the currency icon floats above the machine on a BillboardGui, with the name, the cost and what it gives.
  • Reset pads: the ones that wipe progress (Stack, Obbies, Rebirth, Anchor, Union, Publish, Reflectance, Diamond Plate) also get a red-and-black hazard ring around the base and a confirm pop-up listing what gets reset.

Global build style

  • Scale. 1 pixel on the 2D map = 1 Roblox stud. The whole map is about 1200 × 780 studs. Realm One covers X 0–745, the water channel is 55 studs wide, and The Glacier (Realm Two) covers X 800–1200.
  • Everything is bricks. Floors are studded baseplates. Zone floors sit 1 plate (1.2 studs) above the grey world baseplate, so each zone reads as a coloured plate snapped on top. Each zone also has a 1-stud curb in a darker shade of its colour.
  • Two looks, one world. The ground and buildings are bright toy plastic (SmoothPlastic). The machines are dark slate with Neon glow, so they stand out as the "things you interact with".
  • Stud trail. Oversized yellow studs lead from spawn to every zone in unlock order.
  • Locked zones. A translucent brick wall (Transparency 0.5) with a padlock and "Stack X" text blocks each entrance until it's unlocked.
  • Lighting. Realm One: bright day, ClockTime 14, warm sun. The Glacier: cooler Ambient (#C9E6FF), light Atmosphere haze and falling snow.
