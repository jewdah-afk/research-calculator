Global build style

  • Scale. 1 pixel on the 2D map = 1 Roblox stud. The whole map is about 1200 × 780 studs. Realm One covers X 0–745, the water channel is 55 studs wide, and the Arctic covers X 800–1200. Footprints below use the same numbers.
  • Everything is bricks. Floors are baseplates with visible studs (Studs surface texture, or a tiled stud decal at 1 stud = 1 tile on zone floors). Zone floors are 1 plate (1.2 studs) higher than the grey world baseplate, so every zone reads as a separate coloured plate snapped on top.
  • Materials. SmoothPlastic for bricks, with Glass/Neon only for glowing parts (portal, pads, orbs, flames). Avoid realistic materials (wood, concrete, grass). The world should look like a toy set.
  • Borders. Each zone gets a 1-stud-high curb in a darker shade of its own colour, with 2-stud gaps where the stud trail enters.
  • Stud trail. A path of oversized yellow studs (2×2 studs wide, 1 apart) leads from Spawn to every zone in order, so new players can always follow the yellow dots.
  • Pads. Every stand-on pad is a raised 1-plate tile with a Neon inner square and a BillboardGui showing the name and cost. Pads stay a consistent size (8×8 studs for main pads, 6×6 for small ones).
  • Locked zones. Put a translucent brick wall (Transparency 0.5) with a padlock sign and "Tier X" text across each zone's entrance until the player unlocks it.
  • Lighting. Realm One: bright daytime, ClockTime 14, warm sun, soft shadows. Arctic: cooler Ambient (#c9e6ff), light Atmosphere haze and falling snow particles.
