Stud Incremental Map

Top-down layout of every section, laid out on a grey baseplate. Each zone is a coloured plate, numbered in the order players unlock it. Realm One sits on the left. Crossing the Ascension Portal takes you over the water to the Arctic (Realm Two) plate on the right. Gates come from the real tier unlocks in the game scripts.

Yellow stud trail = walking path from spawn White pedestals = rune packs (currency · unlock) Water = realm border (crossed only via portal)

Realm One · Stud City

1Spawn Plaza

Unlock: start · T0

Spawn point, passive Energy generator and the Tier pad. Everything branches off from here, so keep it central and roomy.

2Brick Forge

Unlock: Tier 1

Flame pad and Realm Points pad. Stand-on pads, so make them big bright tiles.

3Power Press

Unlock: Tier 2

Power pad. Right next to the Forge because Flame feeds Power.

4Rune Pack Row

Beginner/Royal T1 · Basic T3 · Color T6 · Nature T9

One long strip of pack pedestals, ordered left to right by unlock so players walk further as they progress.

5Mob Arena

Unlock: Tier 4

Mob Zone with XP, auto-attack and Loot. Fenced ring in the middle for mob spawns, plus a chest spawn spot.

6Prism Lab

Unlock: Tier 5 (x2 pad + talent prism button T6)

Prisms generator, x2 pad and the Talent Tree board.

7Orb Garden

Unlock: Tier 7

Orbs generation. Sits between Spawn and the Cube Merger since orbs spawn from cubes.

8Cube Merger

Unlock: Tier 8

Merger, Cubes, Accelerator and Gears. A good spot for a big conveyor-style stud machine.

9Sphere Tower

Unlock: Tier 9 (Hail T10)

Spheres levels. Tall build so it's visible from spawn as a goal.

10Challenge Gates

C1 T6 · C2 T8 · C3 1e393 · C4 1e96 Energy

Four doors, C1–C4. Entering resets you to Tier 0 with a restriction; clearing gives a permanent boost.

11Ascension Portal

Unlock: Energy > 1e2283 (~7 h)

On the bridge over the water. Ascension One unlocks Droplets and the Arctic side.

Realm Two · Arctic

12Frost Pack Row

Polychrome A1 · Cryo Freeze3 · Arctic Chromatize 7 · Galactic Chromatize 750

Late-game packs. Nature, Polychrome, Cryo and Arctic carry the Droplets/Prisms runes, so add a sign here as a hint.

13Droplet Well

Unlock: Ascension One (Tier 10+ uses Droplets)

Droplets gen plus the Water and Ice multiplier buttons. Ice needs Freeze 2.

14Freeze Works

F1–3 T11 · F4–6 T12 · F7–9 T13

A 3×3 grid of Freeze pads, one row per tier. Icicles and Haze at T13.

15Chromatizer

Unlock: Prisms_Chromatizer talent (5e43 Prisms)

Chromatize pad. Chromatize 1+ starts Chromium generation.

16Chromify Spire

Chromify 1 Light · 2 Ascended · 3 Reflection

End-game tower. The pads unlock floor by floor as you go up, with the Chroma pad at Chromatize 1000.

Build notes

  • This is a layout sketch only. Sizes are relative, so scale the whole thing up to whatever fits your baseplate in Studio.
  • The numbers follow unlock order. Zones that unlock close together sit close together, so new players never walk far for their next goal.
  • Stud theme ideas: every floor is a studded baseplate, zone borders are 1-stud-high walls in the zone colour, and pads are 2×2 or 4×4 tiles.
  • Realm Two can also be a separate place or a teleport instead of being across the water. The portal spot works for either.
