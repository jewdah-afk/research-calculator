How this is built

  1. A replay of seed 0x5EED1 from the headless Lune sim. The lateral path, speed, obstacle gaps, near-miss boosts and deaths are the sim's own output.
  2. Wall brick colours come from tiers.luau: six saturated bands (lime, magenta, violet, cyan, orange, blue), all outside the reserved red, gold and green hue windows. Red, gold and green appear only on the next threat, rewards and the safe gap.
  3. The bands spiral one revolution per 128 studs of depth (the spec's spiral motif). Bricks breathe in 2-stud voxel steps at 0.25 Hz.
  4. Hierarchy: the player has a dark outline and white rim, so the silhouette never breaks. The next obstacle is the only red object, with a pale edge and chevrons. Its gap carries the green outline. Later obstacles are dark slate, and the tunnel fades to near-black past 256 studs (T6).
  5. Speed lines stay out of the central 40% of the screen and off the player and gap. Camera roll stays within ±6°. Reduced motion turns off roll, streaks and breathing.

Deviation to settle: matching the game means wall bricks run far brighter than the spec's T4 band (0.16–0.28). Speed lines are white instead of T5's 0.06–0.14. The hierarchy is held by outline contrast and hue ownership instead of a luminance cap on the walls.
