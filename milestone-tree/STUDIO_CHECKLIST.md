# Studio checklist

What to click in Roblox Studio after an update, and what you should see. If a step looks wrong, note its number and
copy any red lines from the Output window (View › Output).

## Before you start

* Open `build/MilestoneTree.rbxlx`, or keep `sync.bat` + Rojo connected. An update arrives within a minute; press
  Stop, then Play.
* Optional: *Game Settings › Security › Enable Studio Access to API Services* turns on saving in Studio.
* Press **Play**.

## 1. Start

1. A dark loading card ("THE MILESTONE TREE") shows briefly, then the painted realm map fills the whole screen. No
   blue sky strip shows under the Roblox top bar.
2. The Output window has one line `[MilestoneTree] caps ...` and no red lines.
3. The gold points capsule sits at the top centre. Without API access, a red **NOT SAVING** chip shows under it.
4. On a brand-new save, a **START HERE** cue bobs above M. The READY tray, the portal and the zoom buttons stay
   hidden until the first milestone.

## 2. The map

5. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click to zoom in. `-` / `=` and the zoom buttons zoom too.
6. Hover a node: a tooltip shows after a moment. Right-click a node: the peek card opens with OPEN, and with a hold to
   prestige when the layer is ready. Esc or a click outside closes it.
7. **HOME** flies back to the start view. Press it again for the whole realm.

## 3. A layer's panel

8. Click **M**. The camera flies to it and the panel slides in on the right, with the map still visible in a strip
   on the left.
9. Buy and prestige from the panel: the cards, the big button in the header area and the sub-tabs all work. Hover a
   card for its tooltip.
10. Click another node in the strip: the panel switches layers. Click empty map, press Esc or press X: the panel
    closes and the map is full width again.
11. **TROPHIES** opens the achievements. **OPTIONS** opens the game settings and THIS DEVICE (Motion, Map detail,
    Map labels, Interface size).
12. The game's hotkeys work as on the web: M, P, Ctrl+E (the exploration reset), the arrow keys in Exploration.

## 4. READY, the Multiverse, the end

13. When a layer can prestige, it glows on the map and shows in the READY tray. Clicking a gem opens the layer.
    Holding a gem for 0.6 s prestiges it; a ring fills while you hold.
14. At 185 milestones, click the portal (bottom right) or the rift: the gate card opens. Holding for 1.2 s enters the
    Multiverse, with a flash and the camera flying to the rift. The tag under the points holds to exit or finish.
15. After beating the game, the end screen shows. KEEP GOING returns to the map.

## 5. Phone

16. Open Studio's device emulator and pick a phone in landscape (for example iPhone 14, 844 × 390).
17. Tap P: the panel fills the screen and the map is hidden. A bar at the top shows the layer, gems for other READY
    layers and a close button. Close brings the map back, centred on the layer you had open.

## 6. Saves

18. With API access on: play a bit, press Stop, press Play. Your progress is back. SAVING… and SAVED appear briefly
    under the points when the game saves; it saves by itself every minute and when you leave.

## Removed on purpose

* The old page (the menu down the left) and the CLASSIC PAGE switch.
* The Information and Changelog screens. The credits and the hotkey list are in Options › ABOUT.
